# Patch notes

## 1.2.14 · 2026-10-16

## Pet Atlas: plan your Rift farming

- **Map-exclusive pets** filters out pets with recorded farming locations in other maps. It works together with region, type, search, farmable/MAX and tracker-only filters.
- A separate **Pet Discovery Guide**, opened from the book beside the new filter, lists single-map pets and special sources with suggested acquisition information. Click any row for its pet card and the **+** tracker action.
- Known boss identities are labeled separately from verified pet acquisition sources; missing data stays unconfirmed. Single-map means recorded field locations, not global exclusivity or chest/shop restrictions.
- Pet-type color accents, responsive Atlas filter spacing and a refreshed Rift reminder/opening animation.
- Cached map indexes, fewer idle Atlas repaints, shared marker drawing resources and bounded 60 FPS alert/UI animation reduce avoidable work.

Soul counting and passive capture behavior are unchanged. The app remains free and ad-free, with no premium features or paywalls.

Existing installations can update through the app. New users should download **A2PetTracker-win-Setup.exe**.
